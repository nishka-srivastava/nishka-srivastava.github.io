Business Administration📚

I am a first-year undergraduate student at the University of California, Riverside, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology. I am interested in business strategy, technology, and operations, particularly how organizations use data, communication, and strategic planning to solve problems and improve their performance. My academic interests also include economics, artificial intelligence, and business management. Through my coursework and extracurricular activities, I am continuing to develop my analytical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills. I am particularly interested in exploring careers in go-to-market strategy, product management, and business operations within the technology industry. I enjoy learning about how businesses operate, how teams work together to achieve shared goals, and how technology can be used to improve processes and decision-making. As I begin my undergraduate education, I am looking forward to gaining practical experience, exploring different areas of business, and becoming involved in organizations that align with my professional interests.

Throughout high school, I gained experience in leadership, communication, and organizational management through several extracurricular activities. I cofounded and served as President of my school's SWENext chapter, an initiative associated with the Society of Women Engineers that encourages students to explore opportunities in STEM. In this role, I helped establish the organization, coordinate meetings, communicate with faculty and professional mentors, and lead recruitment efforts that generated over 100 initial student sign-ups and grew the chapter to more than 35 members. I also served as Vice President of Public Relations for the JKYog Toastmasters Youth Club, where I managed communications, organized attendance records, coordinated speech voting, and helped establish a club newsletter. Through Toastmasters, I gained experience in public speaking, meeting coordination, and working with members of different ages and experience levels. These leadership positions taught me the importance of organization, effective communication, and taking initiative when working toward a common objective. They also gave me opportunities to take on responsibilities independently while learning how to collaborate with others and manage multiple tasks.

In addition to my leadership experience, I gained hands-on experience in STEM education and community outreach through my internship at Code For Fun, where I helped teach coding and robotics to more than 60 students across summer camps. My responsibilities included assisting instructors, preparing classroom materials, managing technology and equipment, helping students troubleshoot programming activities, and communicating with parents. I also participated in FIRST Tech Challenge robotics as an outreach team member for Cyber Sparks, where I represented the team at community events, demonstrated robotics projects, and helped develop sponsorship materials. As the Fremont Chapter Lead for Codenovate, a student-run nonprofit organization, I supported outreach and recruitment efforts while teaching introductory workshops in Python, Java, and linear algebra. These experiences allowed me to work with different groups of people, explain technical concepts in accessible ways, and develop practical organizational and teamwork skills. They also strengthened my interest in the intersection of technology, communication, and business. As I continue my education at UC Riverside, I hope to build on these experiences through academic projects, student organizations, internships, and other professional opportunities that allow me to apply my skills in new environments.
